Support Centre

We’re here to help. Find answers or get in touch with the HOLENTRIX LTD team directly.

Contact Support

Have a question, a bug report, or need help with one of our apps or services? Our support team is ready to assist you.

Email us at:  support@holentrix.com

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Please include as much detail as possible in your message, such as the app name, device model, and a description of the issue.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug?

Email support@holentrix.com with the subject line “Bug Report” followed by the app name. Include steps to reproduce the issue, your device details, and any screenshots if possible.

How do I request a feature?

We welcome feature suggestions! Send your ideas to support@holentrix.com with the subject “Feature Request”. We review all requests, and popular suggestions may be included in future updates.

I have a privacy concern. Who should I contact?

For data access, deletion requests, or any privacy-related matter, please email support@holentrix.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject. See our Privacy Policy for full details on your rights.

How do I delete my data?

To request deletion of any personal data we may hold about you, email us at support@holentrix.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”. We will process your request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

I have a business enquiry. Who should I contact?

For partnerships, licensing, or business enquiries, please also reach out to support@holentrix.com and our team will direct your message appropriately.

Our Apps

Our applications are available on major platforms. Visit the relevant app store listing to download, rate, or leave a review — your feedback helps us improve.

If you experience any issues with an app, please contact us at support@holentrix.com rather than leaving a negative review — we are happy to resolve issues quickly.
